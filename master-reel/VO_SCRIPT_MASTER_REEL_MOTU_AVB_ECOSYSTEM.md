# Voiceover — MOTU AVB Series Master Reel, 298 s / 4:58

**English only. No burned-in captions.** Narration is recorded separately and
dropped into `public/vo/voiceover-master-reel.mp3`.

## Tone — and why this one

**The long-form video's blend, not the three-part reels'.** Brief Stage 9's
*"Precise & Technical-but-Accessible"* (The Systems Engineer) carries the body,
opened and closed in *"Warm & Trustworthy"* (The Infrastructure Specialist).

The three reels used the Systems Engineer voice throughout, and that was right
for them: each was a single-product pitch to a viewer who might see only one of
the three. This reel is doing something different. It makes an **argument** —
one engine, three front-ends, one network — and an argument needs a listener
who has agreed to the premise first. The opening thirty seconds are recognition,
not specification ("one room is never the last room"), and the close is an
invitation. The Systems Engineer voice is too cool for either. The long-form
already solved exactly this shape at 898 s, so its blend is inherited rather
than re-invented.

**Delivery: ~155 wpm** — between the long-form's unhurried 150 and the reels'
brisker 165. Faster than the long-form because this is still a reel and the
hook has to land in the first few seconds; slower than the reels because the
thesis segment is carrying a real technical argument and the two price figures
must not be rushed.

**Pacing note:** timecodes are the rendered beat boundaries. Start each line
~0.3 s after its boundary so the visual cut reads first.

## Compliance

- No comparison with, or reference to, any other audio-interface manufacturer
  anywhere in the script.
- No mention of TASCAM.
- No reference to any other Shivansh Electronics brand relationship — the only
  relationship stated is MOTU Authorized Distributor for East and North East India.
- **Both** Market Operating Prices are stated in full, separately, in one
  explicit timestamped moment at 04:38–04:47, before the outro's supporting
  contact narration. Neither figure is rounded, blended into a range, or
  preceded by "starting from", and the two are never summed into a system price.
- The AVB Switch is never presented as a peer-tier fourth interface. It is
  introduced at 03:50 as infrastructure, and the 03:59 line makes its actual
  role explicit: the interfaces already network in pairs on their own, and the
  Switch is what you add beyond that.
- The three interfaces are framed as peers throughout — "peers, not a value
  ladder" at 00:30, and each segment opens by changing the *room*, never by
  moving up a range.
- `www.shivanshelectronics.in` is spoken twice in the closing eleven seconds, on
  top of its 21 on-screen appearances across the reel — more than any other
  single contact detail.
- Every specification quoted is from the VERIFIED column of Brief Stage 8.

**Note on the CTA block.** The dual-MOP paragraph is 84 words on its own and is
not compressible: both figures must be spoken in full, unrounded, with the
distributor designation intact. At 155 wpm that block alone needs ~33 s, and the
CTA window is 28 s. Two options, in order of preference:

1. **Speak the price block at ~175 wpm** — it is a list of figures, and a list
   tolerates a brisker read than an argument does. This fits without changing
   the picture.
2. If the read still feels rushed, extend `cta-price` from 9 s to 12 s and take
   3 s from `cta-climax` (8 s → 5 s) in `src/schedule.ts`. The total stays at
   298 s and no other segment moves.

The picture is deliberately left at option 1's timing, because the climax beat
is where all four products appear together and shortening it weakens the one
shot the whole reel is built toward.
