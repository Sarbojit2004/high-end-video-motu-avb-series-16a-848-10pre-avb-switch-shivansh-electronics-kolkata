# Voiceover — Reel 2: "The Matrix" (MOTU 16A), 178 s / 2:58

**English only. No burned-in captions.** Narration is recorded separately and
dropped into `public/vo/voiceover-reel2.mp3`.

## Tone

**"Precise & Technical-but-Accessible" (The Systems Engineer) as the spine, with
a warmer open and close.** Chosen from Brief Stage 9.

**All three reels use this same tone deliberately**, rather than varying by
product. A viewer may meet the set in any order, or see only one — a consistent
voice is what makes three standalone films read as one line rather than three
unrelated ads. The products differ in geometry, not in character, which is the
whole argument of the ecosystem; giving each its own narrator personality would
quietly contradict that.

**Delivery at reel pace:** brisker than long-form — roughly 165 wpm — but never
rushed through a number. The hook has to land in five seconds. Let the two price
figures sit.

**Pacing note:** each block is budgeted against its beat length with air left
for the cut. Start each line ~0.3 s after the timecode so the visual reads first.

## Compliance

- No comparison with, or reference to, any other audio-interface manufacturer.
- No mention of TASCAM.
- No reference to any other Shivansh brand relationship — the only relationship
  stated is MOTU Authorized Distributor for East and North East India.
- **Both** Market Operating Prices are stated in full and separately, even though
  this reel's subject is the 16A: the dual-category pricing is a fact about the
  whole line, not about whichever product a given reel features.
- Neither figure is rounded, blended into a range, or preceded by "starting from".
- The absence of microphone preamps is framed as the 16A's *specialisation*, never
  as a deficit relative to the 848 or 10pre — Brief Stage 15's "peers, not a value
  ladder" rule applies within the line as strictly as the no-comparison rule
  applies outside it.
- `www.shivanshelectronics.in` is spoken twice in the closing fourteen seconds, on
  top of its 13 on-screen appearances across the reel.
- Every specification quoted is from the VERIFIED column of Brief Stage 8.
