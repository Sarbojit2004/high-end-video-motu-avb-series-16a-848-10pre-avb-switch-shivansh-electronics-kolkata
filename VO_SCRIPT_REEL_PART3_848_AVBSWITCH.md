## Tone direction

**Same blend as Parts 1 and 2 — Precise & Technical-but-Accessible (primary)
with Warm & Trustworthy underneath (secondary)** — held across the series.

Part 3 balances the two and resolves. The first two-thirds is the 848, which is
a control-room product and so returns some of Part 1's warmth (talkback, people
in a live room). The last third turns to the AVB Switch and the full network,
where the register goes back to Precise — this is infrastructure, and the scale
figures should be delivered flatly and confidently, without awe.

The closing CTA is the only place in the entire series where the tone becomes
overtly **consultative**. It must not become salesy: state both prices plainly,
then genuinely invite a conversation, because configuration really does vary.

**Delivery notes:**
- Target **150 wpm**.
- "848" is **"eight-forty-eight"**. "7.1.4" is **"seven-one-four"**.
  "gPTP" spelled out as **"G-P-T-P"**. "AVB" as letters.
- The two prices are **never** run together in one breath. There is a deliberate
  pause between them, and each is attached to its own products.
- Do not call the Switch cheap, entry-level, or a starting point. It is infrastructure.
