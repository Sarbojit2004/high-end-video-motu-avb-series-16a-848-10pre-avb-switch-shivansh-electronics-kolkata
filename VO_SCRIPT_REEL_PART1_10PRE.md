## Tone direction

**Chosen blend: Precise & Technical-but-Accessible (primary) with Warm &
Trustworthy underneath (secondary).**

Why: this is a four-product networked ecosystem sold at a serious infrastructure
price to people who will check the spec sheet. A purely Cinematic & Aspirational
read would overpromise against figures that are already impressive on their own,
and would sit badly against a light, restrained, technical visual grammar. The
Precise register lets the verified numbers — +74 dB, −129 dBu EIN, ~1.8 ms RTL —
carry the persuasion themselves. The Warm layer keeps it from turning into a
datasheet reading: this is a person who has actually tracked a band explaining
why these particular numbers matter in a real room.

**This same tone carries across all three parts** for series cohesion. Part 1
leans slightly warmer (it is about performers and microphones); Part 2 leans
slightly more precise (routing); Part 3 balances both and resolves.

**Delivery notes:**
- Target **150 wpm**. Total word count below is budgeted to that.
- Let the numerals land. Small beat *before* each verified figure, not after.
- No hard sell anywhere in Part 1. The price appears once, stated flatly, as
  information — the full CTA belongs to Part 3.
- Pronounce "10pre" as **"ten-pre"**. "AVB" as letters. "MOTU" as **"MOH-too"**.
- `[pause N.Ns]` markers are silence, already costed into the word budget.

  Distributor of MOTU (Mark of the Unicorn, USA) Interfaces for East and North

  The AVB Switch MOP (Rs. 52,990) is deliberately **not** mentioned here — the
  Switch is not part of Part 1's story, and the two figures are never blended.

  designation. The complete outro CTA belongs to Part 3.
- **Verified figures only.** +74 dB, −129 dBu EIN, −114 dB THD+N, 26 in / 28 out,
  54 channels, 8 rear + 2 front, 8 line outs, 2 headphone outs, 3.9" TFT, 64-ch
  CueMix Pro (64 in / 32 buses), ~1.8 ms RTL, 192 kHz, Thunderbolt 4 / USB4,
  optical expansion +10 mic / +8 line, dual-gigabit Milan-certified AVB.
